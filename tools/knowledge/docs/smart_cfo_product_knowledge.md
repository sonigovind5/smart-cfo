# Smart CFO Product Knowledge

## Overview

Smart CFO is a concept-stage and prototype-stage financial decision-support solution designed for small and medium-sized businesses.

It explores how businesses could improve financial visibility, organise financial information, monitor invoices and bills, support reconciliation, review cash-flow scenarios and receive AI-supported financial commentary.

## Intended Users

Smart CFO is intended primarily for:

- SME founders
- Business owners
- Finance managers
- Accountants
- Fractional CFOs

## Problems Smart CFO Explores

Smart CFO is designed to explore challenges such as:

- fragmented financial information
- limited visibility into current cash position
- manual organisation of transactions
- difficulty monitoring invoices and bills
- time-consuming reconciliation processes
- limited cash-flow scenario planning
- difficulty converting financial information into decision-ready insights

## Potential Workflow Areas

The current Smart CFO concept includes the following potential workflow areas:

1. Financial visibility
2. Transaction organisation
3. Invoice and bill tracking
4. Reconciliation support
5. Cash-flow scenario planning
6. AI-supported financial commentary

## Current Status

Smart CFO is currently a prototype and concept-stage solution.

The current project uses illustrative or synthetic information for demonstration and testing.

Any accounting, banking or other software integrations should be treated as proposed capabilities unless they have been separately validated and implemented.

## Important Limitations

Smart CFO is not:

- a replacement for a qualified accountant
- a replacement for a financial adviser
- a source of tax advice
- a source of legal advice
- a source of investment advice
- a source of lending advice
- a guarantee of future financial performance
- a production accounting platform
- a live banking platform

## Responsible Use

Users should not provide:

- passwords
- banking credentials
- full bank statements
- tax identification details
- confidential customer records
- sensitive personal financial information

Questions involving tax, legal, lending, investment, compliance, security or confirmed implementation commitments should be referred to an appropriate human professional.

## Current Commercial Position

Confirmed pricing, implementation timelines, live integrations and service-level commitments are not yet available in this knowledge document.

The appropriate next step for an interested organisation is an initial discovery conversation or a controlled pilot discussion.
